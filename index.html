Model notes

Simulation mode uses a simplified model fitted to the full-size Simulink results, not the Simulink model itself. Turbine: axial-flow, K = 7.237, efficiency 0.758, maximum power at half of runaway speed (Chapter 5). Generator and charger: buck-boost with MPPT, 17 A constant-current and 56.8 V constant-voltage limits, speed-dependent generator loss. Pressure drop: turbine ρKv²/2 plus pipe and fittings (lower-bound outlet loss). UPS: transfer 17.0 ms, backup voltage 117.2 V (700 W) or 117.3 V (875 W), battery draw 960 W or 1,171 W, return 30 s after the utility is stable (Chapter 6). Flashing operation at 40% state of charge (TxDOT). Backup runtime assumes the turbine keeps charging during the outage at the present flow, and 430 W battery draw in flashing operation. For example 5 ft/s gives about 360 rpm and 210 W of charging, close to the Simulink 394 rpm and 208 W.
